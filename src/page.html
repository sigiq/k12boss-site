Building real software used to take years of training. Now any student can do it. We think that is the biggest opportunity for student success in K-12 right now.

Why this show

The biggest opportunity in K-12 is also its biggest opportunity cost

Schools weren't designed for a world where any student can build software. No test score, ranking or funding formula pushes them to adapt. So change depends on the educators who move first, and on everyone else seeing what they did.

What's possible now

  • Students build real things.Apps, games and tools for their families, schools and towns, in days instead of years.
  • It teaches the skills schools already promise.Problem-solving, creativity, collaboration and communication grow fastest through real work for a real audience.
  • Every student gets to build.You no longer need years of syntax before your idea counts. Computing stops being only for future engineers.
  • New paths open up.A student who can build something end to end has another route to a career, with or without a degree.

What waiting costs

  • Nothing in the system rewards it.Subjects that are tested and funded get taught. This one isn't, so it rarely happens on its own.
  • The gap grows every semester.What students can do with these tools keeps pulling away from what school is set up to teach.
  • Educators can't teach what they haven't seen.Most teachers haven't watched a student build with AI yet. Seeing it is what changes minds.
  • Some kids will learn it anyway.Students with tech-savvy parents will pick this up at home. Everyone else is counting on school.

How every episode works

Real people, honest obstacles, one thing to try

  1. The opportunity

    A classroom, district or program where students are already building with AI.

  2. What's in the way

    Time, money, policy, fear. Our guests say plainly what made it hard.

  3. How they moved anyway

    What they actually did, in their own words, so you can borrow it.

  4. Your turn

    One small project to run with one student or one class this week.

The K12 BOSS Workshop

Every episode comes with homework

Each episode ends with one small project. Try it once with a student or a class, then share what happened with the community. We'll talk about what comes in on the show.

20 minutes · 1 student

The idea audit

Ask a student what they would build if they knew it was possible. Just listen and write it down. Then share the idea with us.

1 class period

Find the gate

Find one place where a student's idea gets stuck on "how." Remove that barrier one time and see what they build.

2 days · Paper, then AI

Paper first

Sketch an app on paper, build it with an AI tool, then hand it to a classmate. Where do they get lost?
